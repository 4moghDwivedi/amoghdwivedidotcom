AMOGH DWIVEDI

Amogh Dwivedi is a musician who realizes his musical thoughts in a variety of ways, be it procedurally generated music in Max/MSP, music written out by hand, or by improvising at the piano. His electronic music has been featured at the International Computer Music Conference 2025, PdMaxCon25~, Performing Media Festival 2025, and Bleep / Blorp Festival. His concert works have been played by Mivos Quartet, Mia Elezoviç, and Iktus Percussion.

Growing up, Amogh learned how to sing Hindustani classical music and how to play the electronic keyboard, and enjoyed listening the music of Linkin Park, Avicii, deadmau5, Avenged Sevenfold, Metallica, Dream Theater, Animals as Leaders and Glenn Gould. His first creative musical experiences were making electronic music in the vein of EDM styles as a teenager.

Beginning at its Valencia campus in Spain, and eventually heading to Boston in 2021, Amogh pursued a double major at Berklee College of Music, studying Composition with Marti Epstein, Ryan Suleiman and Don McDonnell, and Electronic Production and Design with Matthew Davidson, David Cardona, Jason Petrin, and Dr. Richard Boulanger. Alongside working on his majors, Amogh began and sustained an interest in playing the piano, and acquired a firm interest in jazz, studying with Christian Li, Doug Johnson, and Neil Olmstead. He remains invested in his growth as an improvisor. He was presented with the Max Mathews Award and Bill Maloof Award in 2024, and the Leroy Southers Award in 2023. He graduated summa cum laude in May 2024.

At Berklee, Amogh had the privilege of being a tutor for Core Music Tutoring, and later the EPD department, where he discovered his passion for education and talking about music. He also served as the Vice President for the Society of Composers, one of Berklee's oldest student clubs, where he often led weekly meetings, helped organize concerts, and wrote weekly newsletters.

Amogh is currently pursuing his MFA at California Institute of the Arts as a Music Technology student.
